🚗⚡ Got a BYD Dolphin Surf? I built a free tool for you.

Surf4Miles turns the trip data on your car's USB stick into a proper energy report: cost per mile, miles per kWh, how you compare to a petrol or diesel car, and more — all from the EC_database.db file you can copy off your car in a couple of minutes.

The bit I'm most pleased with: there's no account, and nothing about your driving is ever stored on our server. Your data lives only on your own device, and you can back it up or move it between devices (like your phone and the in-car browser) whenever you like.

It's free, it's fan-made, and it's not affiliated with BYD in any way — just something I built for my own car that felt worth sharing with other owners.

👉 https://surf4miles.z-add.co.uk/
